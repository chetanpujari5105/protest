        Methodology & limits

        What do we actually know?

        Awaaz promises auto-updating news coverage as a goal, not “every protest live.” News feeds cannot establish that a gathering is still happening or where a crowd is right now.

        Three separate questions on every listing

        Event status

        Announced · Reported ongoing · Ended · Unknown. Taken only from what sources say.

        Evidence

        News report · Organizer announcement · Unreviewed submission.

        Freshness

        When the source was published, and when our system last checked it. “Checked 2 minutes ago” ≠ “confirmed active 2 minutes ago”.

        Proposed production pipeline

        1. News discovery (GDELT DOC/GEO) + permitted publisher feeds + public announcements — every 15 min on a server scheduler
        2. Extract candidate event details with supporting excerpts
        3. Reject irrelevant stories; separate event date from article date; distinguish past vs future
        4. Group reports about the same event
        5. Human review of new events, location changes, and conflicting claims
        6. Publish cards with summaries, source links, and timestamps — never full copied articles

        Discovery terms: protest, demonstration, dharna, march, strike, आंदोलन, धरना, मोर्चा. These find candidates; they don't prove anything.

        Safety rules

        • No attendee GPS, attendance tracking, participant identities, private meeting points, or moving-crowd tracking.
        • Directions only for explicitly published public venues with current, reviewed information.
        • City-only information gets a coarse marker and no route.
        • Conflicting sources are flagged, not silently resolved.
        • Neutral listing rules across causes and viewpoints. Awaaz is independent and non-partisan.

        Data sources considered

        • GDELT — 15-minute updates; DOC & GEO APIs. Used here for browser-side discovery.
        • ACLED — weekly; for historical context and cross-checking, subject to access terms.
